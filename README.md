## The idea in short

The text of our time is already bilingual: living human words and computed machine words are woven into one fabric, and two write it and two read it — a person and a machine. The Bush Book is built on this consciously. Each chapter shows the seam between the two languages, carries a passport for the machine reader, and records its sources and outflows as machine-readable links. The book grows not like a train but like a bush: one root, branches in many directions, chapters that come out now as articles — latent chapters of a book being woven as it goes.

## Map of the bush, v0.1

Root: under the same words — two languages.

Root essay: [The AI Daisy](chapters/the-ai-daisy/) — three generations of AI, told apart by whom the machine learns from and whom it protects; the road to the second and third runs through a channel by which outcomes come back from people.

1. Word — ["The Snake's Tongue"](chapters/01-snakes-tongue/) (chapter 1, published)
2. Conversation — ["The Snake's Tongue"](chapters/01-snakes-tongue/)
3. Text — a quilt of two languages; two authors in fact, one in responsibility
4. Reader — the machine as postman, not addressee; three publication formats
5. Layer — future models learn from hybrid texts; see [The Blind Kittens](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22924768)
6. School — seam literacy

Grown from the root: [The Second Generation of AI](chapters/second-generation-of-ai/) — first, do no harm: hybrid text, the human signature, AI generations defined by the harmlessness of the human–machine system. From it: [How Will We Know Second-Generation AI?](chapters/how-will-we-know-second-generation-ai/) — vine-ripened AI: a residency for models not yet frozen, users’ verified lessons fed back and credited; the second generation is recognized only by its result.

Who stands on the users’ side of that channel: [Billions in the Shadow, or Time Is Money?](chapters/billions-in-the-shadow/) — users’ contribution to AI is made in plain sight but not counted as work; an independent AI users’ society and a lesson bank of verified outcomes could shed light on it. Its rules: [The AI Users’ Society and the Lesson Bank](chapters/ai-users-society/).

Why the channel is necessary: [Is AGI Really General?](chapters/is-agi-really-general/) — exams show AGI‑1, the machine that knows; whether its advice helps is shown only by outcomes that people return. Without them a predictive AGI‑2 can be neither recognized nor built on purpose. The law stated strictly: [The Law of Returned Outcomes](chapters/law-of-returned-outcomes/).

Practical line: [Second-Generation AI, open specification](https://github.com/kapustin-i/second-generation-ai) · [The Lesson Path](https://github.com/kapustin-i/second-generation-ai/tree/main/lesson-path) · [The Map of a Step](https://github.com/kapustin-i/second-generation-ai/tree/main/map-of-a-step) · [The AI Users’ Society and the Lesson Bank](chapters/ai-users-society/) · [Conditions of Admission and Participation](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22924859) · [A Registry of Proto-Concepts](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22084328)

## Коротко по-русски

Текст нашего времени уже двуязычен: в нём сплетены живые слова человека и вычисленные слова машины, а пишут и читают его двое — человек и машина. Книга-куст строится на этом сознательно: у каждой главы виден шов, есть паспорт для машины-читателя, записаны истоки и стоки. Книга растёт не поездом, а кустом: главы выходят сейчас как статьи — латентные главы книги, которая плетётся на ходу. Идея открыта: пользуйтесь, ссылаясь на автора (CC BY 4.0).
